Sort options (Mixture is the default)

Published date: jobs with a published date come first, newest to oldest. Jobs without one go to the end, ordered by last scanned.
Last scanned date: newest scan first, oldest last.
Mixture: jobs sort by published day, newest first. Jobs published on the same day are ordered by last scanned, newest first. Jobs with no published date go after all the published ones, ordered by last scanned.
